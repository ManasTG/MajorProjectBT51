Important Medical Disclaimer

PulseAI is an academic research prototype. Its predictions are experimental and must not be used for diagnosis, treatment decisions or emergency assessment. Model confidence is not a measure of clinical accuracy. ECG results should be interpreted by a qualified healthcare professional.
