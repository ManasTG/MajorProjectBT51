ABOUT PULSEAI

A Smarter Approach to ECG Data Analysis.

PulseAI is an academic AI-powered patient health monitoring platform designed to explore heartbeat classification through electrocardiogram (ECG) signals.

By combining digital signal processing with machine learning, the platform transforms ECG recordings into structured visual insights for research and demonstration.

  • ✓ ECG signal upload and processing
  • ✓ Machine learning based heartbeat classification
  • ✓ Visual analysis and downloadable reports
